# Self-Improvement Iteration Contract\n\nA self-improvement iteration is an evidence transition, not a score-maximization step.\n\n## Inputs\n\n- observation receipt with exact source, declaration, evidence, and toolchain identities\n- candidate change expressed as a bounded operation\n- verification evidence bound to the candidate change and exact source head\n- explicit rollback condition\n\n## States\n\n- PROPOSED: a candidate exists, but acceptance evidence is incomplete\n- ACCEPTED: the candidate passed its declared verification gate and preserved provenance\n- REJECTED: the candidate falsified its expected change or violated a guardrail\n- UNKNOWN: the earliest required stage cannot be established\n\n## Acceptance invariants\n\nAn iteration may be accepted only when:\n\n1. the observation receipt and candidate share the declared provenance boundary\n2. verification evidence identifies the exact candidate source head\n3. the expected change is falsifiable and the observed delta is recorded\n4. the rollback condition is explicit and evaluable\n5. no execution or authorization is inferred from an observational result\n\nA metric movement is not itself acceptance evidence. If a denominator is zero,\nidentities changed, or a required stage is absent, the result remains UNKNOWN.\n\n## Required outputs\n\nEvery iteration emits:\n\n- candidate identity and source head\n- prior receipt digest\n- verification evidence digest\n- resulting state\n- first missing stage, when UNKNOWN\n- next operation\n- rollback condition\n- non-executing and non-authorizing guardrails\n\nThe next iteration should minimize unresolved evidence while preserving declaration\nbinding, replay continuity, and the original provenance chain.\n
