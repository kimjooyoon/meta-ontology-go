# Non-executing improvement candidate

`candidate.gooo` extends the read-only observation boundary without granting an
executor. The first deterministic policy maps the explicit non-claim
`value-level computation` to a proposed `0/1 -> 1/1` witness experiment.

The generated candidate is data. It contains no command, patch, executor,
mutation, promotion, or automatic-adoption authority. Its one executable-input
binding is a caller-owned, immutable source snapshot with an exact AST span,
semantic activity identity, typed input/expected-output corpus, evaluator
registry digest, and zero allowed effects. A candidate therefore does not mean
that an improvement happened or that the proposal is good.

The Gooo contract is compiled before the Go meta-reducer may emit one candidate.
Unknown source decisions lower resolution and emit zero candidates. Known
integrity failures fail closed at exact resolution and emit zero candidates.
