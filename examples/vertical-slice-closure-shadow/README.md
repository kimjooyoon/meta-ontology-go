## Resolution rules

Missing evidence or an unknown top decision becomes
`FAIL_CLOSED / LOWER_RESOLUTION`. A known failure, subject mismatch, observer
write, or broken digest link becomes `FAIL_CLOSED / INVARIANT_ONLY`.

## Munchhausen choices

- `FOUNDATION`: fixed denominator, merged assurance, and unknown preservation
- `COHERENCE`: six boundaries and twelve transitive links agree
- `REGRESSION`: repository writes and mutation authority remain zero

The positive property is diagnostic locality: a failure is reported at a
specific boundary rather than hidden behind one readiness percentage. No
novelty claim is made.
